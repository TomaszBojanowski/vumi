# Album covers in the screenshots

The screenshots of Vumi (`data/screenshots/` and their copies on the website) show album covers from the
[netlabels collection of the Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/netlabels). The covers belong to
their authors and are used under the licenses below. Vumi's own license (GPL-3.0-or-later) does not apply to
them, and their authors do not endorse Vumi.

**Changes to the covers:** in the screenshots they are scaled down and have rounded corners, and a cover that
is not square has bars around it. A cover marked *part* is also cut off by the edge of the window or by a panel.

"Cover image by" names the author of the picture where the source names someone other than the artist.

The table gives the license in the Internet Archive entry. The covers differ for three albums: Fola shows the BY,
NC and SA icons, Room With a View says Creative Commons 2.5 Attribution, and Arium FL says Creative Commons 2.5.
